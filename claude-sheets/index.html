Claude Sheets

Claude Sheets is a private tool used by one person. It lets their own AI assistant read and edit cells in their own Google Sheets, on request, from their own computer.

What it can do:

  • Read cells and formulas from a spreadsheet.
  • Write values or formulas to a range of cells, after the owner approves each write.
  • Add rows to the bottom of a table, after the owner approves each write.

What it cannot do: delete files, delete tabs, share files, or change who can open them.

It is not offered to anyone else and has no sign-up.
