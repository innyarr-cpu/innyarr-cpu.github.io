Privacy policy – Hermes Drive

Hermes Drive is used by one person, its owner, to give a personal AI assistant read-only access to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It requests read-only Drive access only. It cannot create, edit or delete files.
  • Data read from Drive is used only to answer the owner's own requests. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
